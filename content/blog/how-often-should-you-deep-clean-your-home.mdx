**Most homes need a full deep clean every 3 to 6 months** on top of your regular weekly or fortnightly clean — more often if you have pets, young kids, or anyone in the house with allergies. Here's how to work out where your home actually sits on that scale, and when to book.

## Deep Clean vs Regular Clean

A regular clean keeps the surfaces you see every day under control: floors, benches, bathrooms, general tidiness. A deep clean goes into the areas a regular clean skips — oven interiors, fridge seals, grout lines, skirting boards, window tracks, behind and under furniture. We've broken this down in full in our [guide to what's included in a professional deep clean](/blog/what-is-included-in-professional-deep-clean) — this post is about timing, not the checklist itself.

## How Often to Deep Clean, by Household

| Household type | Recommended frequency |
|---|---|
| Single person or couple, no pets | Every 5–6 months |
| Family with school-age kids | Every 3–4 months |
| Home with pets | Every 3 months |
| Allergy or asthma sufferers in the house | Every 2–3 months |
| Rental you're about to move out of | Before final inspection ([see our end of lease guide](/blog/how-to-prepare-for-end-of-lease-inspection)) |

These are starting points, not rules — a home that gets a lot of foot traffic, cooking, or renovation dust will drift toward the more frequent end regardless of household size.

## Melbourne's Seasons and When to Book

**Late autumn / early winter (May–June):** Windows and doors stay shut for months once winter sets in, which traps dust, pet dander and mould spores indoors. Booking a deep clean now — especially bathroom and window-track mould treatment — means you're not living with it until spring.

**Spring (September–November):** The classic "spring clean" slot, and for good reason — it's the natural point to reset after winter and open the house back up. This is also when pollen tracks in from outside, so carpets and soft furnishings benefit most.

**Before summer (December):** If you host over the holidays, a deep clean in early December covers ovens, entertaining areas and outdoor spaces before the silly season starts.

**After renovations or a move:** Regardless of season, construction dust and previous-owner grime need a deep clean before regular maintenance can keep up.

## Signs Your Home Is Overdue

- A musty smell that airing out the house doesn't fix
- Visible grime building up in grout lines or around taps
- Dust returning within a day or two of a regular clean
- The oven, rangehood filters or fridge seals haven't been touched in months
- Window tracks and skirting boards you can write your name in

If two or more of these sound familiar, it's time to book rather than wait for the "right" season.
