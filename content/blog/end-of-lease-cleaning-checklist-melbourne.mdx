**Agents inspecting a vacated property in Melbourne work through the same core areas every time: kitchen, bathrooms, carpets, windows and general surfaces** — comparing each against your original condition report. Miss one of these and it's the most common reason bonds get held back. Here's the checklist, room by room.

## Kitchen

- Oven cleaned inside and out, including racks and trays
- Rangehood and filter degreased
- Stovetop and splashback free of grease and residue
- Inside and outside of all cupboards and drawers wiped down
- Benchtops, sink and taps cleaned and polished
- Fridge and dishwasher cleaned inside if included in the lease

The oven and rangehood are, by far, the two items most likely to send an inspection back for a re-clean — a quick wipe isn't enough; agents check inside the door seals and under the elements.

## Bathrooms

- Tiles and grout scrubbed, not just surface-wiped
- Shower screen, bath and tapware descaled
- Toilet disinfected inside and out, including the base
- Mirrors and glass polished streak-free
- Exhaust fan dusted

Grout and shower screens are the second-most common fail point — mould or soap scum build-up needs a proper scrub, not a spray-and-wipe.

## Living Areas & Bedrooms

- All floors vacuumed, swept and mopped
- Skirting boards and architraves dusted
- Walls spot-cleaned for scuffs and marks
- Light fittings and ceiling fans dusted
- Wardrobes and built-in cupboards wiped inside and out

## Windows & Tracks

- Glass cleaned inside (and outside where accessible)
- Window sills and tracks cleaned of built-up dirt
- Flyscreens wiped down
- Blinds or curtains dusted

Window tracks collect more dirt than any other spot in the house and are an easy thing to forget — agents check these specifically.

## Carpets & Floors

Most Victorian leases require carpets to be professionally steam cleaned before handover if they were cleaned or new at the start of the tenancy — check your specific lease wording. A receipt from the cleaning company is usually needed as proof for the property manager.

## Outdoor Areas (If Included)

- Garage floor swept
- Balcony or courtyard cleared and swept
- Cobwebs removed from eaves and entryways
