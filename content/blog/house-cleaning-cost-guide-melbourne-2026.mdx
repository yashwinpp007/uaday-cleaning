**A standard residential clean in Melbourne's west starts from around $80** for a smaller property, with end of lease and larger jobs typically running $120–$250 depending on size. Here's what actually drives that price up or down, so a quote makes sense before you book one.

## What Affects the Cost of House Cleaning

- **Home size** — more rooms and bathrooms means more time on site, which is the single biggest cost driver
- **Type of clean** — a regular maintenance clean costs less per visit than a deep clean or end of lease clean
- **Condition of the property** — a home that hasn't been cleaned professionally in a while takes longer on the first visit
- **Frequency** — ongoing weekly or fortnightly clients generally pay less per clean than one-off bookings
- **Add-ons** — carpet steam cleaning, inside-oven, inside-fridge and window cleaning are usually quoted separately from a standard clean

## Price Guide by Home Size

| Home size | Standard clean (approx.) | Deep clean (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 bedroom | $80–$120 | $150–$220 |
| 3–4 bedroom | $120–$180 | $220–$320 |
| 5+ bedroom | $180+ | $320+ |

Bathrooms, not just bedrooms, are a major factor here — a 3-bedroom home with three bathrooms will cost more than one with a single bathroom.

## One-Off vs Ongoing Cleaning: Which Is Cheaper Long-Term?

A one-off clean is priced for exactly what it is — a single visit, usually to a home that needs more attention because it hasn't had a professional clean recently. An ongoing weekly or fortnightly arrangement is priced for maintenance: the cleaner is keeping a standard up rather than resetting it from scratch, which is why regular clients typically pay less per visit than they would for a series of one-off bookings.

If you're deciding between the two, a good rule of thumb: book one deep clean to get the home to a baseline, then move to a regular maintenance schedule from there rather than repeated one-off cleans.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a standard house clean cost in Melbourne?**
Residential cleans in Melbourne's west typically start from $80 for a smaller home, with larger properties costing more depending on size and condition.

**Is end of lease cleaning more expensive than a regular clean?**
Yes — end of lease cleans are more thorough and typically run from $120 to $250 depending on property size, since they cover ovens, tracks, grout and other areas a regular clean skips.

**Does a one-off clean cost more than an ongoing weekly or fortnightly clean?**
Often, yes. A first-time or one-off clean usually takes longer because there's more built-up dirt to deal with, whereas ongoing cleans maintain a standard and tend to be quicker — and cheaper per visit — over time.
