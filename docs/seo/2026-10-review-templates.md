# Review request and reply templates

Never offer discounts or incentives in exchange for reviews, and never edit or invent reviews.

Review link: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJUeNu6EwEvU8RE1ebbrNTeJ0

## SMS (send within 24 hours of the job)
> Hi [first name], thanks for choosing Uday Cleaning. If you're happy with the clean, a quick Google review would really help us: [link]. Thank you!

## Email
> Subject: How did we go?
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for letting us clean [address/suburb]. If you were happy with the result, we'd be grateful if you could leave a short Google review: [link]. It helps other people in Melbourne find us.
>
> If anything wasn't right, reply to this email or call 0420 203 336 and we'll make it right.
>
> Thanks,
> Uday Cleaning

## QR flow
Create a QR code for the review link and add it to the tax invoice and any printed checklist.

## Replies
**Positive**
> Thank you [name]! We're glad you're happy with the [service]. We appreciate you taking the time. – Uday Cleaning

**Negative or mixed**
> Thanks for the feedback, [name], and we're sorry the clean wasn't what you expected. Please call 0420 203 336 so we can understand what happened and put it right.

## Keep in mind
- Mention the service and suburb naturally in replies where it fits.
- Reviews on the site (`lib/data/reviews.ts`) must be verbatim Google reviews. 17 are on GBP, 10 on the site.
